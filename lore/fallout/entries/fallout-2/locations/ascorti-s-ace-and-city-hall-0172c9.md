# Ascorti's Ace and City Hall

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "Redding locations", "Fallout 2 shops"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; Redding locations; Fallout 2 shops

Ascorti's Ace and City Hall is a casino and de facto seat of government in Redding. It offers gambling facilities and a place to convert one's loot into cash and is owned by the mayor, Ascorti.
