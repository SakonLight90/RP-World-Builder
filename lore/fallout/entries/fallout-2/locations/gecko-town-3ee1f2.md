# Gecko (town)

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "Gecko"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; Gecko

Gecko is a Ghoul town northeast of Vault City. Its economy is based on the operation of the old, half-functional Poseidon Energy Atomic Powerplant No. 5 and subsistence agriculture established among the remnants of the town surrounding it. Gecko can be found twenty-two squares east and two squares south of Arroyo.
