# Los Angeles (State)

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "Los Angeles", "Fallout 2 mentioned-only locations"
- categories: Fallout 2 mentioned-only locations
- in this game: mentioned only, it has no entry of its own

Los Angeles was a pre-War city located in Los Angeles County, California. After the war, the ruins of the area were resettled and renamed the Boneyard and a state was named Los Angeles by the New California Republic.
