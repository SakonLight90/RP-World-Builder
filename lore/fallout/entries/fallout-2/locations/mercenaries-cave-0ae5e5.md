# Mercenaries' Cave

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; Mercenaries' cave

## Summary from the source

The Mercenaries' Cave ("Raiders" on the world map) is a very small cave system halfway between Broken Hills and Vault City that forms the base of operations for a group of mercenaries led by Angel-Eyes and currently hired out by the Bishop family.
