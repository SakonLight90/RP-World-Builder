# New California Republic Hospital

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "New California Republic (town)"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; New California Republic (town)

The New California Republic Hospital is the New California Republic's local hospital. Doc Jubilee sells medicines to the player and can also sell a map to find Vault 13 after dialogue with Saltbeef Bob for 10000 NCR dollars, as well as provide medical treatment.
