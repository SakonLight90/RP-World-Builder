# Railway Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "New Reno"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; New Reno

## Summary from the source

The Railway Station is a disused railway station on the East Side of New Reno. It is controlled by the Wright family and serves as the hub of their alcohol operations. It is located at the north end of the block, directly above the Wright family's East Side manor.
