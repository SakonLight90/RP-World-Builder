# Toxic Caves

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "Klamath", "Fallout 2 military and research facilities"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; Klamath; Fallout 2 military and research facilities

The Toxic Caves are north of Klamath. Before the Great War, it was a military warehouse. Now, it's a radioactive cave inhabited by geckos. Smiley, the trapper from Klamath, is waiting at the end of the second level for someone to save him.
