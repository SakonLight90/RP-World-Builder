# Trappers (Fallout 2)

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "Trappers", "Klamath"
- categories: Klamath

Trappers are gecko hunters living in and around Klamath in Fallout 2. They make a living of off trapping geckos, skinning them, and selling their pelts. Their daily life is not an easy one, and they would not hesitate to blackmail people for their own benefits.
