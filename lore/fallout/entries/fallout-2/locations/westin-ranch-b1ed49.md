# Westin Ranch

- type: location
- game: fallout-2
- variants: "New California Republic (town)"
- categories: Fallout 2 locations; New California Republic (town)

## Summary from the source

Westin Ranch is the private residence of Roger Westin. It is located on the west side of the New California Republic Downtown and is only accessible after the guard gives the Chosen One permission to enter and lowers the barrier. For this to occur, one must first have business with Roger Westin or at least convince him of this.
To do so, as it is considered to be private property, the only two ways to gain access are to speak to the Sheriff of NCR for some opportunities for "honest work." After that, it is possible to convince the guard of the ranch to grant entry. Another way to gain entrance is if the Chosen One has low Intelligence (IN < 4). The guard will sigh and say that Westin has a "soft spot" for someone of the Chosen's kind, and grant entry.
