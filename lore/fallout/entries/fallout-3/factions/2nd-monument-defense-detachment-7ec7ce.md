# 2nd Monument Defense Detachment

- type: faction
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 3)"
- categories: Fallout 3 factions; Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 3)

The 2nd Monument Defense Detachment is a squad of the Brotherhood of Steel, assigned to guard the Washington Monument for Galaxy News Radio. It is led by Paladin Berrings.
