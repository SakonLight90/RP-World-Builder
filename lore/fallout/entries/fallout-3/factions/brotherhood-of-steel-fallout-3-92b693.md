# Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 3)

- type: faction
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Brotherhood of Steel"
- categories: Fallout 3 factions; Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 3)

Lyons' Brotherhood of Steel or the Capital Wasteland detachment is a contingent of the Brotherhood of Steel in Washington D.C. The group's headquarters is located in The Citadel, led by Elder Owyn Lyons.
