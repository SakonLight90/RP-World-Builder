# Lyons' Pride

- type: faction
- game: fallout-3
- categories: Fallout 3 factions; Lyons' Pride

Lyons' Pride (commonly abbreviated as the Pride) is a tactical unit comprised of elite soldiers from Lyons' Brotherhood of Steel in Fallout 3. The Pride is tasked with the suppression of Super Mutant activity within the Mall and Chevy Chase areas of Washington, D.C.
