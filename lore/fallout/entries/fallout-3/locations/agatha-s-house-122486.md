# Agatha's House

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Central Plains and Potomac", "Fallout 3 radio facilities"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Central Plains and Potomac; Fallout 3 radio facilities

Agatha's House is a small, single room house in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located west of the Scrapyard and northeast of Meresti Trainyard. It can be easily spotted by the radio transmission tower rising above the boulders that surround the house.
