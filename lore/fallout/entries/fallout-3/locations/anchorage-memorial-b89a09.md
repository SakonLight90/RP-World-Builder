# Anchorage Memorial

- type: location

- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Exterior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins

## Summary from the source

The Anchorage Memorial is a monument located in the southeast quadrant of the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It commemorates the Battle of Anchorage in Alaska. Inside are several structural levels as well as an underground maintenance area which is inhabited by Mirelurks.
