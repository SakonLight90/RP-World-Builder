# Bailey's Crossroads

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Operation: Anchorage locations", "Fallout 3 districts"
- categories: Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Operation: Anchorage locations; Fallout 3 districts

Bailey's Crossroads is a small district of Washington, D.C. in Fallout 3. It is inhabited by Super Mutants and Brotherhood Outcasts. It is available through the Fallout 3 DLC Operation: Anchorage.
