# Bethesda Ruins

- type: location

## Summary from the source

The Bethesda Ruins are located northwest of Washington, D.C. in Fallout 3. They are inhabited by Raiders and Feral Ghouls, and are divided into three sections: Bethesda Offices West, Bethesda Offices East, and Bethesda Underworks.
