# Chaste Acres Dairy Farm

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northeast Territories"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northeast Territories

The Chaste Acres Dairy Farm is the ruin of a small farm in the Capital Wasteland. It is located in the northeastern section of the map, southwest of the Republic of Dave. It was taken over by a group of Raiders.
