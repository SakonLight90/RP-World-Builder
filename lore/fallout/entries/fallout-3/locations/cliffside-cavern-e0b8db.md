# Cliffside Cavern

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts

Cliffside Cavern is a cave system west-northwest of the The Overlook Drive-In in Fallout 3. It is inhabited by Raiders and Yao Guai.
