# Coastal Grotto

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Point Lookout locations"
- categories: Point Lookout locations; Coastal Grotto

The Coastal Grotto is a small cave in Point Lookout in Fallout 3. It is where Plik lives and holds a "safari" of sorts with Ghouls as the hunt. There are also two men, Jacob Humboldt and Rip Smithy, in the safari chamber. It only has one interior section.
