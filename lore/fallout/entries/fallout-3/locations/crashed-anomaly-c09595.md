# Crashed Anomaly

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northern Mountains secondary locations", "Mothership Zeta locations", "Fallout 3 radio facilities"
- categories: Northern Mountains secondary locations; Mothership Zeta locations; Fallout 3 radio facilities

The Crashed Anomaly is an unmarked location in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is equidistant from the Clifftop Shacks to the northwest and Greener Pastures Disposal Site to the southeast.
