# Dunwich Building

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Southwest Territories", "Point Lookout mentioned-only locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Southwest Territories; Point Lookout mentioned-only locations

The Dunwich Building is an office building in the Capital Wasteland. It is located on the southwesternmost point of the map, south of Girdershade and far west of Tenpenny Tower. It has three interior sections including the entrance, Forsaken Dunwich Ruins, and the Virulent Underchambers.
