## Summary from the source

Dupont Circle is located in the northern part of the urban D.C. area, in the southeastern part of the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is accessible via the backdoor of the Galaxy News Radio building, the Collapsed Car Tunnel, or the metro tunnels.
