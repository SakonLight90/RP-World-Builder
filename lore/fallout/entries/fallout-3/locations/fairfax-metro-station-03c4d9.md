# Fairfax Metro Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "White Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; White Line Metro Stations

Fairfax Metro Station is a Metro station in the Capital Wasteland. The station is named after the Fairfax Ruins. Station identification signs are badly worn and are no longer readable. The mezzanine is largely intact, whereas the platform has been heavily damaged. The subway tunnels have been destroyed, and the tracks run north to south.
