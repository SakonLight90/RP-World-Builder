# Fairfax Ruins

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts", "Fallout 3 towns"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts; Fallout 3 towns

Fairfax Ruins are the destroyed shops and dwellings of Fairfax in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located south of Vault 101, east of Fort Independence, and north of Andale.
