# Farragut West Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Red Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Red Line Metro Stations

## Summary from the source

Farragut West Station is a Metro station and part of the old underground Metro tunnel system in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It exits into Friendship Station and the Farragut West Metro Station. The main entry is due east of Super-Duper Mart.
