# Fort Bannister

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Irradiated Western Plains", "Fallout 3 military and research facilities"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Irradiated Western Plains; Fallout 3 military and research facilities

Fort Bannister is a pre-War military installation in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is north of Evergreen Mills, south of Jalbert Brothers Waste Disposal, and west of Bannister Crater.
