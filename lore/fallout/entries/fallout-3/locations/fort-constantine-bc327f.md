# Fort Constantine

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northwest Territories", "Fallout 3 military and research facilities"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northwest Territories; Fallout 3 military and research facilities

Fort Constantine is a large military base in Maryland. It contains an Atomic Bomb storage and launch facility in the northwest Capital Wasteland, between the SatCom Array NW-05a and SatCom Array NN-03d. It is guarded by a wide variety of robots, including Sentry Bots, Mister Gutsys, Robobrains, and Protectrons, inside and out.
