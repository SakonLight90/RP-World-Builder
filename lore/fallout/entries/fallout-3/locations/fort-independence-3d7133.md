## Summary from the source

Fort Independence is the headquarters of the Brotherhood Outcasts in the Capital Wasteland and formerly a pre-War scientific base.  It is located west of Fairfax Ruins and north of Andale. Upon the first arrival, the Brotherhood Outcasts fortifying Fort Independence are being attacked by Raiders from Fairfax Ruins.
