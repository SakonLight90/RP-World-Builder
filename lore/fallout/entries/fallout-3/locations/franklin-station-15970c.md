# Franklin Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Blue Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Blue Line Metro Stations

Franklin Station is a Metro station, and a stop on the Blue Line in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. This station is east of Falls Church Metro and west of Minuteman Metro Station. The DCTA Status Monitor identifies this station as currently active.
