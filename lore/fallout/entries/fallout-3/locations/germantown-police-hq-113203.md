# Germantown Police HQ

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Central Plains and Potomac", "Germantown"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Central Plains and Potomac; Germantown

The Germantown Police HQ is a former police headquarters that is now under the control of Super Mutants in Fallout 3. It is used as a base of operations from which they raid locales such as Big Town, and further use it as a holding place/processing location for their captured prey.
