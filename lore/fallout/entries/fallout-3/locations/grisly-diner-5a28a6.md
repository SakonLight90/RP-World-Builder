# Grisly Diner

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northeast Territories"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northeast Territories

The Grisly Diner is a dilapidated Dot's Diner in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located north of the Temple of the Union and south-west of Relay Tower KX-B8-11.
