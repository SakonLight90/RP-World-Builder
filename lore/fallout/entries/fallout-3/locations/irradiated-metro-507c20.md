# Irradiated Metro

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Exterior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "White Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Exterior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; White Line Metro Stations

The Irradiated Metro is part of the White Line Metro system under the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. These tunnels connect the Capital Wasteland on the east side of the bridge near The Citadel and L'Enfant south.
