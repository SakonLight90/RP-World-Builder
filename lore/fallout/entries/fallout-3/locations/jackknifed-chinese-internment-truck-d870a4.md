# Jackknifed Chinese Internment Truck

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northwest Territories secondary locations"
- categories: Northwest Territories secondary locations

The Jackknifed Chinese Internment Truck is an unmarked location in the Capital Wasteland. It can be found on a highway east from SatCom Array NW-07c and northwest of the Abandoned Car Fort.
