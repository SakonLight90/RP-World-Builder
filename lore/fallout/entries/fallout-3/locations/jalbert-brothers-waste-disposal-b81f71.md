# Jalbert Brothers Waste Disposal

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Irradiated Western Plains"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Irradiated Western Plains

## Summary from the source

Jalbert Brothers Waste Disposal is a radioactive waste disposal in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located northwest of Megaton and Vault 101. While its closest neighbors are Fort Bannister to the south, Everglow National Campground and Rockbreaker's Last Gas to the northwest, VAPL-58 Power Station to the east and just north of the entrance is the Wasteland Gypsy Village.
