# Lil' Tyke Playhouse

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Point Lookout locations", "Fallout 3 playgrounds"
- categories: Point Lookout locations; Fallout 3 playgrounds

The Lil' Tyke Playhouse is a small hut and playground to the southwest of Marguerite's Shack in Fallout 3. Its only inhabitants are Swampfolk.
