# Mama Dolce's

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "People's Liberation Army locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; People's Liberation Army locations

Mama Dolce's is a location in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It can be reached either through the Arlington Metro Station, then east through Arlington National Cemetery, or through the Arlington Falls Church Metro in Falls Church.
