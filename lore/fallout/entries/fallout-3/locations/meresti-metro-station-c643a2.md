# Meresti Metro Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Central Plains and Potomac", "Meresti", "Red Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Central Plains and Potomac; Meresti; Red Line Metro Stations

The Meresti Metro Station is an underground train station in the Capital Wasteland's Washington, D.C. metro. It is home to The Family.
