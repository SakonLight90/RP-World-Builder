# Metro Central

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Red Line Metro Stations", "White Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Red Line Metro Stations; White Line Metro Stations

## Summary from the source

Metro Central, or Metro Center, is part of the underground metro system beneath the Capital Wasteland, and the largest metro station in the region. It is not controlled by any faction, instead being infested by Feral Ghouls and Vicious Dogs. This metro station is the station where the Red Line and the White Line meet.
