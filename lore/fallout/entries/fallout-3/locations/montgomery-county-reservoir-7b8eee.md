# Montgomery County Reservoir

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northern Mountains"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northern Mountains

The Montgomery County Reservoir is an abandoned water treatment plant in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located northwest of the Reclining Groves Resort Homes and a home for up to a half dozen Raiders and, depending on level, a Sentry Bot. There are Ammunition Boxes in the upstairs of the barn and in a silo to the northwest.
