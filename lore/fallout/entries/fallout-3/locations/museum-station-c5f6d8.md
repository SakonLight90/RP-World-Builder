# Museum Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Red Line Metro Stations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Red Line Metro Stations

## Summary from the source

Museum Station is part of the Metro system under The Mall and serves as an underground passage between the Museum of History and the Museum of Technology. It was originally part of the Red Line.
