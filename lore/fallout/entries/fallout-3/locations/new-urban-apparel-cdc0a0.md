# New Urban Apparel

- type: location

## Summary from the source

New Urban Apparel is a store at Tenpenny Tower. It is run by Anthony Ling who mostly sells Pre-War Outfits, as well as a few sets of Combat Armor and Leather Armor. He carries between 200-300 Caps and can repair items (having a skill of 15).
