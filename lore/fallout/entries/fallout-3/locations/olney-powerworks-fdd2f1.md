# Olney Powerworks

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Broken Steel locations", "Old Olney buildings"
- categories: Broken Steel locations; Old Olney buildings

Olney Powerworks is an old power plant accessible through the Olney Sewers in the Fallout 3 DLC Broken Steel. The player character is sent here in order to find a Tesla Coil during the Shock Value quest.
