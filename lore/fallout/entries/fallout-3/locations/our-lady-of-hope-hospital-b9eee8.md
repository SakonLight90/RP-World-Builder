# Our Lady of Hope Hospital

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins

Our Lady of Hope Hospital is located near Vernon Square Station in the ruins of Washington, D.C. in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is a good source for medical supplies and explosives, and it plays a role in the quest Reilly's Rangers.
