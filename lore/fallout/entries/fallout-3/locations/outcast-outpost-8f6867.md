## Summary from the source

The Outcast Outpost (also VSS Facility or VSS Outpost) is a small Brotherhood Outcast compound located in a relatively pristine pre-War military bunker in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. The building itself was under construction, with only the superstructure, elevator, and the VSS Bunker completed. It is the primary location for the Fallout 3 DLC Operation: Anchorage.
