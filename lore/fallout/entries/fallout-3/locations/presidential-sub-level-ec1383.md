# Presidential Sub Level

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Broken Steel locations", "Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels"
- categories: Broken Steel locations; Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels

The Presidential Sub Level is an underground area in the Fallout 3 DLC Broken Steel. It connects the utility area below the White House with the Presidential Metro which has exits to the Capitol Building's east wing and Adams Air Force Base.
