# Radio Tower

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "The Pitt locations", "Fallout 3 radio facilities"
- categories: The Pitt locations; Fallout 3 radio facilities

The Radio Tower is a location in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It was added in the Fallout 3 DLC The Pitt. It is the source of Wernher's Distress Signal.
