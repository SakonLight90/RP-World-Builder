# Raid Shack

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- categories: Fallout 3 locations

## Summary from the source

The Raid Shack is an unmarked location in the Capital Wasteland occupied by Raiders in Fallout 3. It can be found due east of the Bethesda Ruins, north of the Chryslus Building, and south of the Wheaton Armory.
