## Summary from the source

The Red Racer Factory is a pre-War Red Racer tricycle factory on the outskirts of southwestern Washington, D.C.. It is situated northeast of the Nuka-Cola Plant and south-southwest of Grayditch in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3.
