# Rho Camp

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Fallout 3 Enclave locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 Enclave locations

Rho Camp or Camp RHO is a small Enclave camp in the east of the Capital Wasteland near Canterbury Commons in Fallout 3. It is northwest of Vault 108 and southwest of the Robot Repair Center. It is an unmarked location, and like all Enclave Camps, it does not appear until after The Waters of Life.
