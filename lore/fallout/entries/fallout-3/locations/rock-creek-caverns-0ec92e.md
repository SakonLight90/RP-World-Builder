# Rock Creek Caverns

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Eastern Hills and D.C. Outskirts", "Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Eastern Hills and D.C. Outskirts; Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels

## Summary from the source

The Rock Creek Caverns are a location in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. The caverns compose of a large disconnected metro tunnel/cavern that is inhabited by Mirelurks, Mirelurk Hunters, and/or Mirelurk Kings, depending on level. The caverns consist of a single interior chamber.
