# Ruined Farmhouse

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- categories: Fallout 3 locations

## Summary from the source

The Ruined Farmhouse is a farm west of Washington, D.C. in Virginia. This farm is on the major road linking Springvale to the Super-Duper Mart. The closest settlement is Megaton to the west and to the southeast of this farm, there is a water tower.
