# Scrapyard

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Eastern Hills and D.C. Outskirts"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Eastern Hills and D.C. Outskirts; Scrapyard

The Scrapyard is a salvage yard in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. Aside from heaps of junk vehicles, it is the place where Dogmeat (a possible Companion) will first be encountered, fighting a small band of Raiders.
