# Shalebridge

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Irradiated Western Plains"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Irradiated Western Plains

Shalebridge is a location in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located due north of Broadcast Tower KT8 and slightly southwest of the Abandoned Car Fort. It consists of a broken relay tower, the Shalebridge tunnels, and Shalebridge Hill. The latter two are home to rival Ant colonies.
