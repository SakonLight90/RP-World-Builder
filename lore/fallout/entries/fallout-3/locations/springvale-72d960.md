# Springvale

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Southern Plains and D.C. Outskirts; Springvale

Springvale is located west of Washington, D.C. in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. Springvale School, Megaton, and Vault 101 are all close to the location.
