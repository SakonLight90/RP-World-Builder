# Statesman Hotel

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins

## Summary from the source

The Statesman Hotel is a twelve-story building in Vernon Square in the ruins of Washington, D.C. To reach the majority of this building, one must go through Our Lady of Hope Hospital and cross the fallen radio mast. The building is infested with Super Mutants and Centaurs. Reilly's Rangers (except for Reilly) can be found stranded on the rooftop, pinned down by Super Mutants.
