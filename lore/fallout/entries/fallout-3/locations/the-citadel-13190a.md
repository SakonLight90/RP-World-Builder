# The Citadel

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Citadel", "Exterior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Fallout 3 military and research facilities", "Fallout 3 towns"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Exterior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Citadel; Fallout 3 military and research facilities; Fallout 3 towns

The Citadel, sometimes referred to as the DC Bunker, is the headquarters of the Brotherhood of Steel in the Capital Wasteland. It is the former Pentagon, located on the west end of the Key Bridge, northeast of Arlington Library.
