# The Pitt Underground

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Pitt Underground", "The Pitt locations"
- categories: The Pitt locations

## Summary from the source

The Pitt Underground is an area in the Fallout 3 DLC The Pitt. It contains the power plant and can be accessed from the Steelyard, near a wrecked train entering an inaccessible metro tunnel. The second exit leads to Haven's courtyard in Uptown.
