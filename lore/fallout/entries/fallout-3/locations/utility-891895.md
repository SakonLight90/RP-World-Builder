# Utility

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels

The Utility area in Washington, D.C. connects a manhole in Pennsylvania Avenue with the remains of the White House via a tunnel. In the DLC Broken Steel, it also contains an access to the Presidential Sub Level which leads to the Presidential Metro with exits to The Capitol Building's east wing and Adams Air Force Base.
