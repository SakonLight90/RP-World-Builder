# Vault 112

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Fallout 3 Vault locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Fallout 3 Vault locations

Vault 112 is a Vault-Tec Vault located in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located in the southwestern part of the region, underneath Smith Casey's Garage, west of Evergreen Mills and south of the Charnel House.
