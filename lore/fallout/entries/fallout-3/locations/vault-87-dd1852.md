# Vault 87

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Irradiated Western Plains", "Fallout 3 Vault locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Irradiated Western Plains; Fallout 3 Vault locations; Vault 87

Vault 87 is a Vault-Tec Vault located in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is located northwest of Little Lamplight and southwest of Broadcast Tower KT8. It is the source of Super Mutants in the region.
