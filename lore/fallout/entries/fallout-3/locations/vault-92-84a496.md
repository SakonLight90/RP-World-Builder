# Vault 92

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northeast Territories", "Fallout 3 Vault locations"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northeast Territories; Fallout 3 Vault locations; Vault 92

Vault 92 is a Vault-Tec Vault located in the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. The world's best musicians were invited to Vault 92 with the promise to "preserve musical talent" during the Great War. In reality, this Vault was geared specifically for experimenting with white noise generators that implanted subliminal messages into the minds of its resident population. It is located northwest of the Deathclaw-infested ruins of Old Olney.
