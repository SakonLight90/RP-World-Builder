# Vernon East Takoma Park

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins", "Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Interior D.C. Metropolitan Ruins; Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels

Vernon East Takoma Park is a tunnel in the partly caved in Metro. It gives a route from Vernon Square to Takoma Park.
