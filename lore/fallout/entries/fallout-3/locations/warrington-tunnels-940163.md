# Warrington Tunnels

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels", "Warrington Station"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Fallout 3 Metro Tunnels; Warrington Station

The Warrington Tunnels are a series of ruined metro tunnels that run between Warrington Station and Warrington Trainyard. The tunnels are populated by a dozen Feral Ghouls and leads to Roy Phillips and his gang's hideout.
