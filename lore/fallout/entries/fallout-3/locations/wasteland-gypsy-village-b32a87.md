# Wasteland Gypsy Village

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Irradiated Western Plains", "Fallout 3 towns"
- categories: Irradiated Western Plains; Fallout 3 towns

The Wasteland Gypsy Village is an unmarked location in the Capital Wasteland, situated north of the Jalbert Brothers Waste Disposal. There are four shacks in total, three of which can be accessed.
