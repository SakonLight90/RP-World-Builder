# WKML Broadcast Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-3
- variants: "Northwest Territories", "Fallout 3 radio facilities"
- categories: Fallout 3 locations; Northwest Territories; Fallout 3 radio facilities

The WKML Broadcast Station is located in the northwest section of the Capital Wasteland in Fallout 3. It is situated just south of Fort Constantine, north of Faded Pomp Estates, northwest of Silver Lining Drive-In, west of Drowned Devil's Crossing, and east of Dickerson Tabernacle Chapel.
