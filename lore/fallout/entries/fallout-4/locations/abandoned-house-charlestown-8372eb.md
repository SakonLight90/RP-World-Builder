# Abandoned House (Charlestown)

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Fallout 4 locations
- variants: "Abandoned House"

This Abandoned House is an unmarked location in the Charlestown neighborhood of Boston in Fallout 4. It is infested with Feral Ghouls. The easiest path to the house is to turn right at the end of the bridge leading towards Bunker Hill, and then take the first left.
