## Summary from the source

The Boston Police Rationing Site is a location in The Commonwealth in Fallout 4. It was used by the Boston Police Department to distribute supplies to civilians but came under investigation by the United States Army for ration shortages. The supplies were delivered by NH&M via a rail line, which connects to the NH&M Freight Depot a short distance to the east.
