# Choice Chops

- type: location

## Summary from the source

Choice Chops is an unmarked location in Diamond City in Fallout 4. It is owned and operated by Polly as a butcher shop selling cuts of meat from a wide range of animals. The shop specializes in Brahmin meat, sourced from Codman Family Farms.
