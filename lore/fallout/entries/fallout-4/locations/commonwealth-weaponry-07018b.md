## Summary from the source

Commonwealth Weaponry is an unmarked location in the Diamond City market in Fallout 4. It is owned and operated by Arturo Rodriguez as a weapon shop offering "hand-tooled" weapons, ammunition, weapon modifications, and various recommendations to passersby and interested clients. The shop is stated to be open "8 days" per week.
