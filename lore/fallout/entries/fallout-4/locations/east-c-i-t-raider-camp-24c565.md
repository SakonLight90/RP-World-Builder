# East C.I.T. Raider Camp

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Fallout 4 locations

## Summary from the source

The East C.I.T. Raider Camp is an unmarked location in The Commonwealth in Fallout 4. Listed simply as "Office Building" on the local map, it can be found between the C.I.T. Ruins and Greenetech Genetics.
