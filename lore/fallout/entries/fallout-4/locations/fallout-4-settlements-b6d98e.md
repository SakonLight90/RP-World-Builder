# Fallout 4 Settlements

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Fallout 4 settlements

In Fallout 4, the Sole Survivor can build and manage their own settlements at 37 sites around The Commonwealth. The workshop interface is used to place and connect pre-fabricated structures as well as individual pieces (walls, floors, roofs, etc.) letting the player character construct their own bases.
