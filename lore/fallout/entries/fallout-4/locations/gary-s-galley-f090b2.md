# Gary's Galley

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Rivet City"
- categories: Rivet City

## Summary from the source

Gary's Galley is a restaurant in Rivet City run by Gary Staley, the owner and cook, and his daughter Angela. When Rivet City's marketplace opens in the morning, most shop owners can be found eating here. Other inhabitants will eat here as well at varying times of the day.
