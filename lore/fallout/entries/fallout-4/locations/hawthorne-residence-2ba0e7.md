# Hawthorne Residence

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Diamond City buildings", "Fallout 4 Residences"
- categories: Diamond City buildings; Fallout 4 Residences

The Hawthorne Residence is an unmarked location in Diamond City in Fallout 4. It belongs to Hawthorne and Eustace Hawthorne, two of the more affluent residents of Diamond City.
