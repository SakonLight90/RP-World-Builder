# Hub 360

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Theater District secondary locations

Hub 360 is an unmarked location in the Theater District of Boston in Fallout 4. It is directly west of the Harbormaster Hotel and south of the Fallen Skybridge. Hub 360 can also be reached south of Water Street Apartments, through an alleyway and multiple back-end stairwells.
