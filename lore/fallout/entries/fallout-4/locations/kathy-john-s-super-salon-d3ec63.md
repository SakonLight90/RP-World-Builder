# Kathy & John's Super Salon

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Diamond City buildings", "Fallout 4 shops"
- categories: Diamond City buildings; Fallout 4 shops

Kathy and John's Super Salon is an unmarked location in the Diamond City market in Fallout 4. It is owned and operated by the mother-and-son cosmetology duo Cathy and John as a barbershop offering the latest hairstyles from the upper stands and across the Commonwealth. The salon serves the beauty needs of both travelers and city residents.
