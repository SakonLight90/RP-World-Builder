# Lil' Gentry Playground

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Fallout 4 Parks"
- categories: Fallout 4 Parks

Lil' Gentry Playground is an unmarked location in the Beacon Hill neighborhood of Boston in Fallout 4. It is northeast of Vault-Tec Regional HQ and west of the Boston Bugle Building.
