# Lucky 38 Basement

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Lucky 38"
- categories: Lucky 38

The Lucky 38 Basement is the basement in the Lucky 38 Casino in Fallout: New Vegas. Here, Mr. House (or Yes Man, if the Courier chooses to take over New Vegas), demonstrates the abilities of the Securitron MKII units to the Courier.
