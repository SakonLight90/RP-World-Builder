# Marowski's Chem Lab

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Fallout 4 locations

Marowski's Chem Lab is an unmarked location in the South Boston neighborhood of Boston in Fallout 4. It is accessible by a catwalk on the roof of the Four Leaf Fishpacking Plant leading to a hidden security door opened via a Master-locked terminal, or by tripping the lasers outside the plant as per Trish's Note.
