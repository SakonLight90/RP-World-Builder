# Nuka Rockets

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Nuka-World rides"
- categories: Nuka-World rides

Nuka Rockets is a ride in the Nuka-World Amusement Park and is a part of the Galactic Zone and Kiddie Kingdom in Fallout 4. It is located southwest of the RobCo Battlezone in the Galactic Zone, and west of the Fun House in Kiddie Kingdom.
