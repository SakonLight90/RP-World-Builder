# Nuka-World Power Plant

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Nuka-World locations", "Nuka-World exterior locations"
- categories: Nuka-World locations; Nuka-World exterior locations

The Nuka-World Power Plant is a location in the Nuka-World Amusement Park in the Fallout 4 DLC Nuka-World. It is located directly west of the Galactic Zone, in the northwest part of the map. It can be seen from a distance as it is atop a small rocky hill.
