# Poseidon Energy (Fallout 4)

- type: location

- variants: "Poseidon Energy", "Southern Commonwealth primary locations"

Poseidon Energy is a location in The Commonwealth in Fallout 4. It was formerly a power plant, which used natural gas and solar energy, owned by the Poseidon Energy corporation.
