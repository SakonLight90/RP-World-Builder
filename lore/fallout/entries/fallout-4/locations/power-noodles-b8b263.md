# Power Noodles

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Diamond City buildings"
- categories: Diamond City buildings

Power Noodles is an unmarked location in the Diamond City market in Fallout 4. It is operated by the Protectron Takahashi, and was founded 15 years ago. Takahashi sells noodles for 55 Caps per bowl.
