# Schoolhouse

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Diamond City buildings"
- categories: Diamond City buildings

## Summary from the source

The Schoolhouse is an unmarked location in Diamond City in Fallout 4. Here, the children of Diamond City are educated by Mister Zwicky and his robotic teacher's aid Miss Edna, who is a Miss Nanny. The school is open 24 hours, with some young students entering and leaving throughout the day.
