# The Nucleus

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Nucleus", "Far Harbor locations"
- categories: Far Harbor locations; Children of Atom locations; Far Harbor Children of Atom locations

The Nucleus (formerly Mount Desert Island Naval Facility) is a pre-War United States Navy submarine base in the Fallout 4 DLC Far Harbor. DiMA made it his home and allowed the Children of Atom to settle the location after he left. In return for the new home and center of worship, they agreed to protect his old memories from being accessed.
