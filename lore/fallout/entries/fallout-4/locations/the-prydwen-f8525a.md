# The Prydwen

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Prydwen", "Coastal Commonwealth primary locations"
- categories: Coastal Commonwealth primary locations; Fallout 4 locations

The Prydwen is a location in The Commonwealth in Fallout 4. The flagship of the Brotherhood of Steel, the Prydwen serves multiple roles for the Brotherhood, including those of aircraft carrier, command center, clinic, personnel quarters, equipment maintenance bay and research facility. It will be moored to the air traffic control tower at Boston Airport shortly after the Sole Survivor exits Fort Hagen during the quest Reunions.
