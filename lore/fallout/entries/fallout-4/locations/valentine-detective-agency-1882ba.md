# Valentine Detective Agency

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Diamond City buildings", "Fallout 4 Post-War Offices"
- categories: Diamond City buildings; Fallout 4 Post-War Offices

Valentine Detective Agency is an unmarked location in Diamond City in Fallout 4. It is owned and operated by Nick Valentine, who uses the office as the base for his private investigation business together with his secretary Ellie Perkins.
