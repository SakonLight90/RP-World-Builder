# Vault 76

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- categories: Vault 76

Vault 76 is a Vault-Tec facility located in The Forest region of Appalachia. It is located north of Flatwoods and was designated as the "Official Vault of the Tricentennial" by Vault-Tec, under the tagline "Vault-Tec Salutes America."
