## Summary from the source

Waypoint Echo is a location in the Commonwealth in Fallout 4. Located between the Edge of the Glowing Sea and Vault 95, it only becomes active if the Sole Survivor is allied with the Brotherhood of Steel and helps them reactivate Liberty Prime during the quest Liberty Reprimed.
