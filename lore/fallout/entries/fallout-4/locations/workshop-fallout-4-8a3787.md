# Workshop (Fallout 4)

- type: location
- game: fallout-4
- variants: "Workshop", "Fallout 4 settlement objects"
- categories: Fallout 4 settlement objects

The workshops are part of the crafting system in Fallout 4 and deals with buildings and furniture at settlements. The Sole Survivor can use the workshop to move, deconstruct, or create new objects from scrapped material. These objects can be used to create thriving settlements with turrets, walls, vendors, and recreational spots.
