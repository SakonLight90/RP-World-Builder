## Summary from the source

The Yangtze or Yangtze-31 is a Liao-Class Type 113 Chinese nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine commanded by Captain Zao.  When first discovered by the Sole Survivor, it is lodged in the shallow waters of Boston Harbor south of Boston Airport and northeast of the Four Leaf Fishpacking Plant.
