# Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76
- categories: Fallout 76 factions

## Summary from the source

The Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force, currently referred to as the Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel, is a faction comprising Brotherhood of Steel soldiers sent from Lost Hills to re-establish contact with the original Appalachian Brotherhood. Ever since reaching West Virginia, they have taken the mantle of their brothers and sisters in protecting and gathering technology from all around Appalachia.
