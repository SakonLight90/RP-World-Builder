# Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Brotherhood of Steel"
- categories: Fallout 76 factions

The Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel, also known as the West Virginia Brotherhood or West Virginia Chapter, was a founding chapter of the Brotherhood of Steel under the command of Paladin Elizabeth Taggerdy. By late 2095, they had been entirely wiped out by the Scorched; however, a small group of reinforcements later arrived from Lost Hills in 2103, and established a new Brotherhood chapter in the area.
