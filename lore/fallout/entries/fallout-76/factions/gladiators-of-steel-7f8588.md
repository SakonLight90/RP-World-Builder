# Gladiators of Steel

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76

## Summary from the source

The Gladiators of Steel, or the Brotherhood field team, are a faction in Fallout 76. This group of Brotherhood of Steel operating in Appalachia do battle against the Rust Eagles' killer robots in the Metal Dome arena during Event: Test Your Metal.
