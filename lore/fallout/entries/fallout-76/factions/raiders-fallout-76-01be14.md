# Raiders (Fallout 76)

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Raiders"
- categories: Fallout 76 factions

## Summary from the source

The raiders of Appalachia were an alliance of ruthless marauders and bandits from across the region. They consisted of five gangs: the Cutthroats, the Diehards, the Blackwater Bandits, the Gourmands and the Trappers. By 2102, the alliance had been dissolved and its members wiped out. As of 2103, new raiders have begun to arrive in Appalachia.
