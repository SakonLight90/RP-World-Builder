# Responders

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76
- categories: Fallout 76 factions

## Summary from the source

The Responders were a volunteer-based crisis response team and government that operated in Appalachia. Formed shortly after the Great War by local first responder units, they were dedicated to helping local communities survive. They instituted a variety of programs that ranged from basic outdoor survival training to a specialized armed division called the Fire Breathers. Like the other regional factions, the Responders were wiped out by the Scorched Plague. The group was restarted in 2104 to assist needy Appalachians in surviving.
