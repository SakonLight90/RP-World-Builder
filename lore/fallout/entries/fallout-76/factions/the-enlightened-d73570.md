# The Enlightened

- type: faction
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Enlightened"
- categories: Fallout 76 factions

## Summary from the source

The Enlightened, or the Wise Mothman's Enlightened, are a religious faction in Appalachia. They were originally members of the Cult of the Mothman, but splintered from it. They exclusively venerate the purple-eyed Wise Mothman.
