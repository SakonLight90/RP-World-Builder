# Big Bend Tunnel East

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Cranberry Bog locations"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Cranberry Bog locations

## Summary from the source

Big Bend Tunnel East is a location which lies on the Red Line in the Cranberry Bog region of Appalachia. It is the eastern entrance to the rest of the Big Bend Tunnel.  It is the location of a compound inhabited by the Blue Ridge Caravan Company. Inhabitants include Vinny Costa who organizes the caravans through the tunnel, the merchants Eugenie, Carver Timmerman, and Rudy Fernandez, and Blue Ridge guards Kieran Kennedy, Libby Wen, and Aries.
