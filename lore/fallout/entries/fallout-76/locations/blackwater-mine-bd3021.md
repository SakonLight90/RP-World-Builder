# Blackwater Mine

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Savage Divide locations"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Savage Divide locations

Blackwater Mine is a location in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia in Fallout 76. It is located in White Sulphur Springs, West Virginia, and served as a Uranium mine before the Great War.
