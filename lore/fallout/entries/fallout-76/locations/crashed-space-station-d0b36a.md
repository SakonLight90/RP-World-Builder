# Crashed Space Station

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- categories: Fallout 76 locations

The Crashed Space Station, also demarcated the Valiant-1 and referred to by Munch as Nova-Orbital-01, was a United States Space Administration space station that once orbited Earth before the Great War. In the Wastelanders update, the location was renamed The Crater, and used as the headquarters of the Raiders faction.
