# Enclave Research Facility

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Fallout 76 medical theme locations"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Fallout 76 medical theme locations

Enclave Research Facility Site J or the Enclave Research Facility, is a location beneath Transmission Station 1AT-U03 in The Forest region of Appalachia. It was formerly utilized by the Appalachian division of the Enclave.
