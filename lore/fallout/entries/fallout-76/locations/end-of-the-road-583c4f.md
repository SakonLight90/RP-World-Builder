# End of the Road

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Savage Divide locations"
- categories: Savage Divide locations

## Summary from the source

The End of the Road is an unmarked location in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia. It is the wreckage of a truck and its trailer, situated west of the Sunnytop Station and far north of the Monongah Mine.
