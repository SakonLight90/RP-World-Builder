# Fort Atlas

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Fallout 76 dungeons", "Fallout 76 military and research facilities", "Fallout 76 military theme locations"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Fallout 76 dungeons; Fallout 76 military and research facilities; Fallout 76 military theme locations; Fort Atlas

Fort Atlas is a location in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia, formerly known as ATLAS Observatory. Once the site of an advanced military science project, the observatory lay dormant for years following the Great War, inhabited by hostile robots. The Brotherhood First Expeditionary Force set up base at the observatory with the help of Russell Dorsey and renamed it Fort Atlas.
