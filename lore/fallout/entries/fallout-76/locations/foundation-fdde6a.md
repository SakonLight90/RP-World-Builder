# Foundation

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Savage Divide locations", "Donation Box Locations", "Fallout 76 towns"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Savage Divide locations; Donation Box Locations; Fallout 76 towns

Foundation is a location in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia in Fallout 76, introduced in the Wastelanders update. It is the Settler headquarters, and was built on the site of the former Spruce Knob Workshop.
