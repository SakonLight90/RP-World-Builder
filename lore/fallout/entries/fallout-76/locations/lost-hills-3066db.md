# Lost Hills

- type: location

- variants: "Fallout 76 mentioned-only locations"

Lost Hills is a location in Fallout. It is located four squares west and eight squares south of Vault 13. Claimed by soldiers led by Captain Roger Maxson shortly after the Great War, it is the headquarters of the Brotherhood of Steel.
