# Mysterious Cave (Fallout 76)

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Mysterious Cave", "Savage Divide locations"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations; Savage Divide locations

The Mysterious Cave is a location in Fallout 76, introduced in the Wastelanders update. It is located the Savage Divide region of Appalachia, northwest of the Bailey Family Cabin.
