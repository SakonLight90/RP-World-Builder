# The Whitespring Bunker

- type: location
- game: fallout-76
- variants: "Whitespring Bunker"
- categories: Fallout 76 locations

The Whitespring Congressional Bunker (code name  CB_002), also known as Whitespring Shelter, is a location underneath The Whitespring Grounds in the Savage Divide. It is the primary headquarters of the Enclave in Appalachia.
