## Summary from the source

The Treehouse Village was a location in The Mire region of Appalachia. Formerly a settlement established by the Free States, Raiders attacked the survivors taking refuge there before eventually taking over and later abandoning the treehouses. A group of Villagers led by Jennie Brown revamped the abandoned settlement and renamed it to The Retreat.
