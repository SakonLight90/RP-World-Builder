## Summary from the source

Vault 94 is a Vault-Tec Vault located on the border of the Savage Divide and The Mire regions of Appalachia. It was first made accessible as part of Vault Raids, though the raids were discontinued and the Vault was closed with the release of Wastelanders. Vault 94 was brought back as part of the Daily Ops feature in One Wasteland For All. Outside of Daily Ops, the Vault was unlocked in the world and can be explored at will.
