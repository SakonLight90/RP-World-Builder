## Summary from the source

Vault 96 is a Vault-Tec Vault in the Savage Divide region of Appalachia. It is located to the south of Foundation and southeast of R&G Station, at the end of Route 103.
Made up of a small team of brilliant scientists, Vault 96 was advertised as an ark to preserve flora and fauna for the future. However, upon the Vault's sealing, it was revealed that the staff would also be forced to perform experiments relating to genetics and mutations, in order to meet "quotas" set by the Vault mainframe. While a failed escape attempt killed the original residents, and the Vault's cryogenics technology eventually froze over the Vault, Vault 96 later became the site of more sinister scientific research, this time, by former West-Tek employee Dr. Edgar Blackburn, who used the Vault's resources for his work on FEV.
