## Summary from the source

Destroy the Mutant Leader is a main quest in Fallout.
The Vault Dweller has brought the Water Chip back to Vault 13, but the mutant armies still pose a dangerous threat to the safety of the vault. It is up to the Vault Dweller to stop their leader, the Master.
