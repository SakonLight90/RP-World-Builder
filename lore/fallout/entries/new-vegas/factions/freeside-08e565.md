# Freeside

- type: faction
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas factions

## Summary from the source

Freeside is outside of New Vegas in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located directly adjacent to the Strip situated around the intersection of pre-War Fremont Street and Las Vegas Boulevard. Including the immediately surrounding area, it is ruled by the Kings and the Van Graffs. The streets are dangerous and lack the luster of the Strip. There is a conflict between the locals and squatters that has recently followed the NCR to the area.
