# Mr. House

- type: faction
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas factions

## Summary from the source

Mr. House, formally Robert Edwin House, is the Founder, President, and CEO of RobCo Industries, a vast computer and robotics corporation before the Great War. As of 2281, he is the sole proprietor of the New Vegas Strip in the Mojave Wasteland.
