# Abandoned BoS Bunker

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Dead Money locations", "East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Dead Money locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations

The Abandoned BoS Bunker is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is the starting point for the DLC Dead Money. It is located slightly southeast of Camp Forlorn Hope.
