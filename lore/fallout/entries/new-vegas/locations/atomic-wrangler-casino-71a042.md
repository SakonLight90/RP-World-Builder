# Atomic Wrangler Casino

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Freeside locations", "New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Freeside locations; New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations

The Atomic Wrangler Casino is a casino in Freeside, situated across the street from the Silver Rush on the pre-War Fremont Street. It is owned by the twins Francine and James Garret.
