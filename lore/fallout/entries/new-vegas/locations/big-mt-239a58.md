# Big MT

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Old World Blues locations"
- categories: Old World Blues locations; Big MT

Big MT, also known as the Big MT Research Facility or Big Empty, was a pre-War scientific research center and defense contractor located south of Hopeville in Fallout: New Vegas. The facility and surrounding area serves as the game world for the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Old World Blues.
