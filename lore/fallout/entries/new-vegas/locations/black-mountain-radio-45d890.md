# Black Mountain Radio

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Black Mountain"
- categories: Black Mountain

Black Mountain Radio, also known as Radio Free Utobitha, or the Utobithian News Channel, is broadcasted from the radio station at Black Mountain in Fallout: New Vegas. The Courier can discover the radio's origin by following its signal to the top of Black Mountain, where they will discover the Broadcast Building. It is run by Tabitha, who takes on the persona of Rhonda (the name of her broken Mister Handy companion), interviewing herself as "Best Friend Tabitha." Tabitha describes various sects of the super mutant population, talks of the short-comings of the human race, and the wonders of the centaur.
