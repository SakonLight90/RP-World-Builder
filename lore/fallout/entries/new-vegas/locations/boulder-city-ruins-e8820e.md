## Summary from the source

The Boulder City Ruins is a location in the center of Boulder City, between the 188 Trading Post and Hoover Dam in Fallout: New Vegas. The ruins are blocked off from the rest of the city by rubble and a NCR barricade. Lieutenant Monroe stands in front of the barricade.
