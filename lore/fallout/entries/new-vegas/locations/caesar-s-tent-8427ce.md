# Caesar's Tent

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Caesar's Legion locations", "The Fort"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Caesar's Legion locations; The Fort

Caesar's Tent is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located at the summit of Fortification Hill and is where Caesar can be found.
