## Summary from the source

Camp Golf is an New California Republic military base, located on the north shore of Lake Las Vegas, east of the Grub n' Gulp Rest Stop. It is the headquarters for the NCR Rangers in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas.
