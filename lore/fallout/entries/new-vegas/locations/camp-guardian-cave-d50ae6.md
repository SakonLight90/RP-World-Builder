## Summary from the source

The Camp Guardian Cave is a unmarked location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. The two entrances to Camp Guardian cave are found on the path leading up to Guardian Peak. This cave should not be confused with the much larger Camp Guardian Caves. The location is mentioned briefly in the Camp Guardian daily logs found on the path leading to its lower entrance.
