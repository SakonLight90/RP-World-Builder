# Camp McCarran

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Camp McCarran

## Summary from the source

Camp McCarran, also known as McCarran Airport and before the War as McCarran International Airport, is a pre-War airport which serves as the headquarters for the New California Republic Army in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located south of the Strip North Gate and southwest from NCR Sharecropper Farms.
