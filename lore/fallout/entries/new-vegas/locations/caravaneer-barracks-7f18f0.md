# Caravaneer Barracks

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations

The Caravaneer Barracks is a location within the Crimson Caravan Company in Fallout: New Vegas. It is home to the Caravaneers and serves as a place for them to rest and relax after working in the Mojave Wasteland.
