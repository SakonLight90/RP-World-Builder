# Carlyle St. Clair's House

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Fallout: New Vegas Residences", "Northwest Mountains"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Fallout: New Vegas Residences; Northwest Mountains secondary locations

Carlyle St. Clair's House is the home of Carlyle St. Clair III in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located to the north of the Crimson Caravan Company, adjacent to a string of power line towers beside the road running from Horowitz Farmstead to Raul's Shack.
