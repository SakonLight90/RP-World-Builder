# Cell Block A and B

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Powder Gangers locations", "NCR Correctional Facility"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Powder Gangers locations; NCR Correctional Facility

Cell Block A and B are two buildings inside the NCR Correctional Facility in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. They house several Powder Gangers, including Carter, who serves as a merchant.
