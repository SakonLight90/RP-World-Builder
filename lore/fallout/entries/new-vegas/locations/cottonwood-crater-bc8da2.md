# Cottonwood Crater

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations

The Cottonwood crater is an atomic bomb impact point in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is connected to Fire Root Cavern on the north and located due east of Searchlight Airport.
