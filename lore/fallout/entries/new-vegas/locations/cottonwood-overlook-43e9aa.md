# Cottonwood Overlook

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations

The Cottonwood Overlook is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. The once-popular scenic overlook is now an abandoned promontory, offering an unobstructed view of the Legion camp at Cottonwood Cove below.
