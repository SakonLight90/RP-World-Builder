# Crashed B-29

- type: location

- variants: "Mojave Northeast territories primary locations", "Boomers"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Mojave Northeast territories primary locations; Boomers

The Crashed B-29, dubbed The Lady in the Water or The Lady by the Boomers, is a B-29 lying at the bottom of Lake Mead, close to Lake Mead Cave and Callville Bay.
