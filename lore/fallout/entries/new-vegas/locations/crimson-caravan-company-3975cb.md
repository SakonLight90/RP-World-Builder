# Crimson Caravan Company

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations

The Crimson Caravan Company is the New Vegas branch of the Crimson Caravan, currently under the management of Alice McLafferty in Fallout: New Vegas. Located in the Mojave Wasteland, it is southeast of Freeside's North Gate, northeast of the Freeside's East Gate and west of the New Vegas Medical Clinic.
