## Summary from the source

Dead Wind Cavern is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located near Primm Pass and south of REPCONN Test Site, past Harper's Shack. It is home to a wide variety of Deathclaws including the Legendary Deathclaw.
