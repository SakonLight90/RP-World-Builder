# East Pump Station

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations

## Summary from the source

The East Pump Station is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located between Vault 34 and the NCR Sharecropper Farms, just west of the monorail track system. Two entrances to the East Central Sewers are nearby: one east of the building and another northwest near some traffic cones and barrels.
