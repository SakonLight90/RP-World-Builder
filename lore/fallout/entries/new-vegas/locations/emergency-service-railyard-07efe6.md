# Emergency Service Railyard

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Central Mountains primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Central Mountains primary locations

The Emergency Service Railyard is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is found southbound down the railroad tracks from the NCR Correctional Facility, with Primm Pass located in the hills to the east.
