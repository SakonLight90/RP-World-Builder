## Summary from the source

Fire Root Cavern is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. The north entrance of the cavern is located southeast of Searchlight East Gold Mine and southwest of Smith Mesa Prospector Camp. The south entrance connects to the Cottonwood Crater.
