## Summary from the source

The Forbidden Zone Dome Entrance, known as the X-42 Robo-Warfare Facility before the exile of Doctor Mobius, is a location in the northeast section of Big MT in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Old World Blues. The door to this facility is locked until the player character has spoken to Doctor Klein as part of the main quest line Old World Blues.
