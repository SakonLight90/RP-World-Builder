## Summary from the source

The Gate to Long 15 is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located on the southwestern edge of the Mojave Outpost. It allows access to Long 15 if the Courier chooses to launch the nukes against the NCR during the completion of the Fallout: New Vegas DLC, Lonesome Road.
