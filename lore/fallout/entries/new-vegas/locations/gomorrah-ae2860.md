## Summary from the source

Gomorrah Hotel and Casino is one of the few remaining casinos on the New Vegas Strip in Fallout: New Vegas. It is a favorite of NCR Troopers, who often gather in large numbers outside. The casino is managed by the Omertas, one of the Three Families working under the oversight of Mr. House in Fallout: New Vegas.
