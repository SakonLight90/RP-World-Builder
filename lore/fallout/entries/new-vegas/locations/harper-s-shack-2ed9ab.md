# Harper's Shack

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Central Mountains primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Central Mountains primary locations

Harper's Shack is a building in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located to the north of Dead Wind Cavern at the end of Primm Pass.
