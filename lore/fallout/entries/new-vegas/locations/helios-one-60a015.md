## Summary from the source

HELIOS One is a pre-War concentrated solar energy plant consisting of an array of reflectors and the power plant itself. The Poseidon Energy plant houses steam turbines and machines required to transform the energy into usable electricity. It also contains the control system for two experimental satellite weapon systems, ARCHIMEDES and ARCHIMEDES II.
