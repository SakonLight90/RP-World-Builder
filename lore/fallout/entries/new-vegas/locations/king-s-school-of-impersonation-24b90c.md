# King's School of Impersonation

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Freeside locations", "New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Freeside locations; New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations

King's School of Impersonation is a building in Freeside. It is  located on the intersection of Fremont Street and Las Vegas Boulevard, southeast of the Atomic Wrangler Casino and northwest of Mick & Ralph's. The building serves as the home of the Kings and their leader, The King.
