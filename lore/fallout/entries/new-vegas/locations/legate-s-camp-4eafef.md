## Summary from the source

The Legate's Camp is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located east of Hoover Dam. It is surrounded by cliffs and mountains on all sides and is inaccessible for the majority of the game. The only way to enter the camp without using exploits is during the final leg of the main quest. Other than that, the camp remains inaccessible.
