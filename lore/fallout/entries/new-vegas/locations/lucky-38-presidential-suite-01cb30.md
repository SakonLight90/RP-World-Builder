# Lucky 38 Presidential Suite

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Fallout: New Vegas safehouses"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Fallout: New Vegas safehouses

The Lucky 38 Presidential Suite, also referred to as the High Roller Suite by Victor, is player character housing in the Lucky 38 casino. The location is not to be confused with The Tops High Roller Suite.
