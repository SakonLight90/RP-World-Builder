# Mesquite Mountains Lean-To

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Southwest Desert"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Southwest Desert secondary locations

The Mesquite Mountains Lean-To is an unmarked location in the Mojave Wasteland. It is located west by southwest of the California Sunset Drive-in and due east of Mesquite Mountains Crater.
