# Monte Carlo Suites

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations

The Monte Carlo Suites is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is situated on the western side of New Vegas, directly north of Sunset Sarsaparilla Headquarters and south of Westside. It is the base of the Scorpions.
