# Navarro

Navarro (also known as Navarro Refinery and Camp Navarro) is a pre-War installation, where a Poseidon Energy refinery and an Enclave military base are located, located north of San Francisco along the coastline. Navarro was subsequently overrun by the New California Republic, its inhabitants killed. Navarro can be found fifteen squares south of Arroyo in Fallout 2.
