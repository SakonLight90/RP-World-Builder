# NCR Military Police HQ

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "New Vegas Strip"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; New Vegas Strip

The NCR Military Police HQ (also referred to as NCR Military Police Headquarters) is the base of operations for the NCR Military Police on the New Vegas Strip in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located across from the NCR Embassy at the southeast end of the Strip.
