# NCR Tent

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations

The NCR Tents are two tents to the western side of Primm in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. Lieutenant Hayes can be found inside one of the tents alongside a NCR Trooper, and the other tent is occupied by Sergeant McGee.
