## Summary from the source

The NCRCF Prison Break, was a revolt made by the convicts held at the NCR Correctional Facility against the NCR Army troopers who were stationed there to guard them. After the uprising, the convicts fragmented, with some taking over the Bison Steve Hotel in Primm, a larger group moving into Vault 19 to fight against the NCR, while many remained at the correctional facility.
