# Nellis Air Force Base

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Mojave Northeast territories primary locations", "Nellis Air Force Base buildings"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Mojave Northeast territories primary locations; Nellis Air Force Base; Nellis Air Force Base buildings

The Nellis Air Force Base, also known as the Nellis homeland, is a location northeast of New Vegas in the Mojave Wasteland. After the Great War, the base became home to the Boomers, a group originally inhabiting Vault 34.
