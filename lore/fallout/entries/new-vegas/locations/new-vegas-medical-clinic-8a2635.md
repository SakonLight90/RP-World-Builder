# New Vegas Medical Clinic

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Followers of the Apocalypse locations", "Fallout: New Vegas Post-War Health Care Institutions"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Followers of the Apocalypse locations; Fallout: New Vegas Post-War Health Care Institutions; New Vegas Medical Clinic

The New Vegas Medical Clinic is a Followers of the Apocalypse medical facility outside of New Vegas run by Doctor Usanagi. It is located east and right next of Crimson Caravan Company and somewhat southwest of the Mole Rat Ranch.
