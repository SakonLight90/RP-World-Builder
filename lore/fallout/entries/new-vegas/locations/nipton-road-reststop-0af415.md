# Nipton Road Reststop

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Poseidon Gas Stations", "Southwest Desert primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Poseidon Gas Stations; Southwest Desert primary locations

The Nipton Road Reststop is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is a poseidon gas station located on the junction of I-15 and Nevada 164, near Nipton Junction south of Primm.
