## Summary from the source

The Northern Passage is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located north of North Vegas Square. It is the starting point for the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Honest Hearts, as well as a checkpoint for the path to Zion Canyon.
