# Novac

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Central Mountains primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Central Mountains primary locations; Novac

Novac is an independent town in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located south of the Gibson Scrap Yard and north of the Viper's encampment along Highway 95.
