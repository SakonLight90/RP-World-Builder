# Remnants Bunker

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Northwest Mountains primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Northwest Mountains primary locations; Remnants Bunker

The Remnants Bunker is a small Enclave outpost in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It formerly served as a Vertibird refueling station. It is located near Ranger Station Foxtrot, due east of Jacobstown, the entrance of which leads to Pine Ravines Pathway.
