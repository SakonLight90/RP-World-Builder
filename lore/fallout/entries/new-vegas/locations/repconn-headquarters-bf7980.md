# REPCONN Headquarters

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "REPCONN locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; REPCONN locations

REPCONN Headquarters is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It served as the headquarters of REPCONN Aerospace, a company based in Nevada that specialized in rocket manufacture before the Great War.
