# REPCONN Test Site

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Central Mountains primary locations", "REPCONN locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Central Mountains primary locations; REPCONN locations

The REPCONN Test Site is a former rocket testing facility located west of Novac and south of Scorpion Gulch. Its inhabitants consist of Feral Ghouls, Bright Followers, and in the basement level, Nightkin.
