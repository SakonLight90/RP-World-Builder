# Ruined Cafe

## Summary from the source

The Ruined Cafe is a location in the Sierra Madre in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Dead Money. Located inside Puesta del Sol South, the ruined cafe is a safe haven from the Cloud. The ground entrance is just southeast of the western door to Puesta del Sol North.
