# Saturnite Alloy Research Facility

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Old World Blues locations"
- categories: Old World Blues locations

The Saturnite Alloy Research Facility is a location in the southwest of the Big MT in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Old World Blues. It is located southeast of the Z-43 Innovative Toxins Plant and is a factory based on top of a cave system where there is an exposed lava flow.
