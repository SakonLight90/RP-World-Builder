# Sealed Train Tunnel (Central)

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Sealed Train Tunnel", "Fallout: New Vegas Tunnels"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Fallout: New Vegas Tunnels

The Sealed Train Tunnel is an unmarked location in the Mojave Wasteland. It is located at the end of the railroad tracks, west-southwest of Ranger Station Charlie, and is inhabited by Vipers. The nearest fast travel location is Harper's Shack, located in the northwestern hills above the train tunnel.
