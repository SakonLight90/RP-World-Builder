# Searchlight Airport

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations

The Searchlight Airport is a pre-War airport in the southeastern section of the Mojave Wasteland, directly south of Camp Searchlight in Fallout: New Vegas. Its only entrance is a collapsed tunnel on its northern side.
