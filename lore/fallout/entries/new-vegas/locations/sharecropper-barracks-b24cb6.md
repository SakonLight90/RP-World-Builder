# Sharecropper Barracks

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "NCR Sharecropper Farms"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; NCR Sharecropper Farms

The Sharecropper Barracks is a location inside the NCR Sharecropper Farms, located south of Freeside's East Gate in the Mojave Wasteland. It is the office and sleeping quarter for the sharecroppers who work on the land and in the nearby greenhouses.
