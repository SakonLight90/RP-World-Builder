# Sierra Madre

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Dead Money locations"
- categories: Dead Money locations; Sierra Madre

The Sierra Madre is a pre-War location consisting of a city and casino resort. It is surrounded by a poisonous Cloud. Prospectors have sought out the legendary city after hearing a pre-War radio broadcast but did not return. The Courier is lured to the Sierra Madre by the broadcast advertising the Sierra Madre Gala Event.
