# Sierra Madre Vault

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Dead Money locations", "Sierra Madre"
- categories: Dead Money locations; Sierra Madre

The Sierra Madre Vault is a location in the Sierra Madre Casino in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Dead Money. It is the last location the Courier will go before the end of Dead Money. They must proceed through the lower levels of the casino, passing areas filled with the Cloud, speakers, and holograms to end up in the main vault room.
