# Silver Rush

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Freeside locations", "New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations", "Fallout: New Vegas shops"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Freeside locations; New Vegas Conurbation Freeside primary locations; Fallout: New Vegas shops

The Silver Rush is a location in Freeside, and is the largest retailer of energy weapons in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located opposite of the Atomic Wrangler Casino on Fremont Street.
