## Summary from the source

The South Vegas Ruins West Entrance is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is the western entrance into the South Vegas Ruins, located northeast of Poseidon Gas Station and the Violet and Violetta Trailer Compound.
