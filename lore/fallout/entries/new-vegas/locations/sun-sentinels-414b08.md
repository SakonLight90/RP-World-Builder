# Sun Sentinels

- type: location

## Summary from the source

The Sun Sentinels are a location in Zion Canyon in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Honest Hearts. They are located on top of the rocks in the center of the canyon, northeast of North Fork Campground, and west and slightly south of Zion Fishing Lodge.
