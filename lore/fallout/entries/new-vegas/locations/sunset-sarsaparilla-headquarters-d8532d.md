# Sunset Sarsaparilla Headquarters

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations

## Summary from the source

Sunset Sarsaparilla Headquarters is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It was the headquarters of the Sunset Sarsaparilla Company before the Great War. It is located northwest of El Rey Motel and directly west of the NCR Sharecropper Farms.
