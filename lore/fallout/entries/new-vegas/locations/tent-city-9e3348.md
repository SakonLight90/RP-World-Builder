# Tent City

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations

## Summary from the source

Tent City is an unmarked location at Camp McCarran. The tents are located north of First Recon's Tents and the Storage Tent. It is located west of the Medical Tent and the Mess Hall Tent. All of these tents are sleeping quarters for NCR Troopers, except for Tent Four which is a dining area.
