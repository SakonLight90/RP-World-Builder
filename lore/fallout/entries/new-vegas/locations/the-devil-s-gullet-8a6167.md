# The Devil's Gullet

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Devil's Gullet", "Southwest Desert primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; Southwest Desert primary locations

The Devil's Gullet is a location in the Mojave Wasteland. It is located to the northeast of Goodsprings Cave, just over the hill, accessed by descending atop the burned-out cars and a crashed trailer.
