# The Divide

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Divide", "Lonesome Road locations"
- categories: Lonesome Road locations

## Summary from the source

The Divide is a common term for a stretch of devastated land in the northwestern Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located near the California/Nevada border, with Big MT situated to the south. It is the setting of Lonesome Road.
