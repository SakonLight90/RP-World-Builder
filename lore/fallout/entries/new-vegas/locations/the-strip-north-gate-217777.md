## Summary from the source

The Strip North Gate is a location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is one of only two ways to enter the Strip. The only other way to gain access is via the Las Vegas Boulevard Station. However, the Courier will need to have at least a "Liked" reputation by the NCR to enter through the LVB station.
