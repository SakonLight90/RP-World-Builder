# The Think Tank

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Think Tank", "Old World Blues locations"
- categories: Old World Blues locations

## Summary from the source

The Think Tank is a location in the Big MT in Fallout: New Vegas. The building itself is in the shape of a dome, surrounded with bright upward-facing bluish-colored projectors. It contains the residence of the five Big MT scientists, as well as the former apartment of Dr. Mobius, known as the Sink.
