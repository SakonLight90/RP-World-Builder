# The Tops

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Tops", "New Vegas Conurbation New Vegas primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; New Vegas Conurbation New Vegas primary locations

The Tops Hotel and Casino was one of the establishments of the Las Vegas Strip before the Great War. Though damaged by the War, it was restored by Mr. House and the Chairmen, becoming a main attraction.
