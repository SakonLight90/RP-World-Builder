# Train Tunnel (Fallout: New Vegas)

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Train Tunnel", "Mojave Northeast territories primary locations", "Fallout: New Vegas Tunnels"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas unmarked locations; Mojave Northeast territories primary locations; Fallout: New Vegas Tunnels

The Train Tunnel is an unmarked location in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located between Raul's Shack and the Brotherhood of Steel Safehouse, and north of Gypsum Train Yard.
