## Summary from the source

Vault 22 is a Vault-Tec Vault in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. The Vault door is open, with various kinds of plants around the exterior and creatures such as Giant Mantises. It is located and can be approached from south-southwest of the Griffin Wares Sacked Caravan, east of Jacobstown and west from the Thorn.
