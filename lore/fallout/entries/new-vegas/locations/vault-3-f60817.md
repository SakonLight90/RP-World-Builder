## Summary from the source

Vault 3 is one of the Vaults constructed by the Vault-Tec Corporation in the Mojave Wasteland. It was constructed in the southwestern part of Las Vegas, on the outskirts of the city's industrial sector. Save for some flooding in the lower levels, the Vault is still in working condition in Fallout: New Vegas. It is occupied by the Fiends, who killed all of the Vault's original occupants. The Fiends use Vault 3 as a secure fortress and drug den.
