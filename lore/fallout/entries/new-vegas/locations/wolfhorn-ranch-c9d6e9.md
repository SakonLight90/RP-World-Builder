# Wolfhorn Ranch

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations"
- categories: Fallout: New Vegas locations; Fallout: New Vegas marked locations; East Cliffs and South Rad Zone primary locations

Wolfhorn Ranch is a farmstead in the Mojave Wasteland in Fallout: New Vegas. It is located east of Nipton, just south of where Nevada 164 divides to Ranger Station Charlie in the north and to Camp Searchlight in the south.
