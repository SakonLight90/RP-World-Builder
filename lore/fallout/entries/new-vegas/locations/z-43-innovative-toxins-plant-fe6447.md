# Z-43 Innovative Toxins Plant

- type: location
- game: new-vegas
- variants: "Old World Blues locations"
- categories: Old World Blues locations

The Z-43 Innovative Toxins Plant is a location in Big MT in the Fallout: New Vegas DLC Old World Blues. It is located northwest of the Saturnite alloy research facility, west of the X-12 Research Center and east of the X-66 Hexcrete Archipelago.
